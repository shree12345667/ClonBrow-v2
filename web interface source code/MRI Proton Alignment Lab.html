Zeeman Effect & Resonance

Without an external magnetic field, hydrogen protons spin on random axes. Their magnetic fields cancel out ($M_z = 0$).

When exposed to a strong $B_0$ field, quantum mechanics dictates they split into two distinct energy states: Parallel (Spin-Up) or Anti-Parallel (Spin-Down).

Slightly more protons align in the lower-energy parallel state. This tiny excess builds the Net Magnetization Vector ($M_z$) used to generate MRI images.

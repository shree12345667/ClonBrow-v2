Reflex Mechanics

The Patellar Reflex tests the integrity of the L3 and L4 spinal levels.

1. Detection Muscle spindles in the quadriceps detect the sudden stretch caused by the hammer strike.
2. Afferent Transfer A sensory signal travels at approx. 60 m/s to the dorsal root of the spinal cord.
3. Efferent Response The signal synapses directly with a motor neuron, returning to the quad to cause contraction.

Mechanisms of Aging

Every time a cell replicates, its DNA must be copied. However, the enzyme DNA polymerase cannot copy the very end of the lagging strand.

The End-Replication Problem Without telomeres, essential genes would be lost during division. Telomeres act as "buffer zones"—meaningless sequences that can be safely sacrificed.
Crisis State reached. The telomeres are too short to protect the DNA. DNA damage response (DDR) is now constitutive. The cell has stopped dividing to prevent cancer.

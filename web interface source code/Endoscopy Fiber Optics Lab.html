Total Internal Reflection

θc = arcsin(n₂ / n₁)

To act as a perfect mirror, an optical fiber requires a high-index core ($n_1$) surrounded by a lower-index cladding ($n_2$).

When light strikes the boundary, we measure the Angle of Incidence ($\theta_i$) relative to the normal (vertical) line. If $\theta_i$ is greater than the Critical Angle ($\theta_c$), it cannot refract out and is 100% reflected inward.

Medical endoscopes use bundles of tens of thousands of these fibers. One bundle transmits bright Xenon light in, while another carries the reflected image back to a digital sensor.

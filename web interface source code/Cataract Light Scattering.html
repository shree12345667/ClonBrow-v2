Cataract Optics

A cataract is a clouding of the normally clear lens of the eye, typically caused by proteins clumping together over time.

Status: Healthy Lens
The lens is perfectly clear. Light rays pass through undisturbed and converge sharply on the retina.
E F P T O Z
Physics of Scattering: In a healthy eye, the lens refracts light to a single focal point. A cloudy lens acts like frosted glass, causing random multidirectional light scattering (dispersion), which reduces contrast and creates blurry vision with glare.

EEG Superposition Lab

Raw Electroencephalogram (EEG) signals are complex because they are the superposition (sum) of multiple simple sine waves generated by millions of neurons.

Superposition Principle:
When two waves occupy the same space, their net amplitude is the sum of their individual amplitudes. Y(t) = A·sin(2πf₁t) + B·sin(2πf₂t)

Beta Wave (Fast)

Biological Context:
In an awake state, fast, low-amplitude Beta waves dominate. As you close your eyes to sleep, slower, higher-amplitude Alpha waves begin to synchronize and summate across the cortex.

Mechanics of Bionic Sight

Visual prostheses bypass damaged photoreceptors (like in Macular Degeneration) and directly stimulate surviving retinal ganglion cells or the visual cortex.

Phosphene Generation Patients do not see normal images. They see "Phosphenes"—flashes of light created by each individual electrode firing.
The Resolution Bottleneck Normal human vision uses ~100 million photoreceptors. Early bionic eyes (like the Argus II) had only 60 electrodes. Reading large text requires at least 1,000 electrodes.

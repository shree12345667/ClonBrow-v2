Capillary Action

Observe how blood defies gravity and climbs up a narrow glass tube during a finger-prick test.

Jurin's Law
h = (2γ cosθ) / (ρgr)
h = Height of fluid rise
γ (gamma) = Surface Tension
r = Tube Radius (The key variable)
Status: High Adhesion
The tube is narrow. The adhesive forces between the blood and the glass wall easily overpower the gravitational weight of the fluid column.
The Physics: Adhesion pulls the liquid up the sides of the glass (forming a concave meniscus), while surface tension holds the surface intact, pulling the rest of the fluid up with it. The narrower the tube, the higher it climbs!

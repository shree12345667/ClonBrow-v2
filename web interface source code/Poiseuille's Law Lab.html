Hemodynamics Lab

Explore how blood viscosity (thickness) forces the heart to increase pumping pressure to maintain flow.

Poiseuille's Law (Rearranged)
ΔP = η × (8·L·Q) / (π·r⁴)
ΔP = Pressure required from Heart
η (eta) = Blood Viscosity
Q = Constant Blood Flow
Status: Normotension
Viscosity is normal. The heart easily maintains a standard pressure of 120 mmHg to perfuse the tissues.
Clinical Context: Conditions like Hyperglycemia (excess blood sugar) or Polycythemia (excess red blood cells) make blood "syrupy" and thick. To push this thicker fluid at the same rate, the heart must generate drastically higher pressure, leading to Hypertension.

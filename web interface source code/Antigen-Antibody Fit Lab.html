Epitope vs. Paratope

Epitope (Viral Spike)

The specific part of an antigen that is recognized by the immune system. Different viruses evolve different geometries to evade detection.

Paratope (Antibody Tip)

The binding site on an antibody. It is highly variable, allowing for the trillions of combinations needed to fight diverse pathogens.

Neutralization

Once bound, antibodies physically block the virus from entering host cells and "flag" the pathogen for destruction by macrophages.

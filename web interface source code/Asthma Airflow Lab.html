Bernoulli's Principle

To maintain a constant flow rate ($Q$) through a narrowed tube, the air must accelerate ($V$ increases).

According to Bernoulli's equation, an increase in fluid velocity results in a decrease in static pressure.

Dynamic Airway Collapse!

The extreme pressure drop (suction) inside the airway pulls the floppy walls inward during exhalation, causing air trapping and wheezing.

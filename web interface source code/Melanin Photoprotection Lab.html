The Molecular Parasol

Melanin acts as a physical shield, absorbing high-energy UV photons and safely dissipating them as heat.

Supranuclear Cap Melanosomes are transported via dendrites to keratinocytes, where they cluster directly above the nucleus, creating a microscopic parasol to shadow the DNA.
Pyrimidine Dimers When UV radiation bypasses the melanin shield, it strikes the DNA, causing adjacent Thymine bases to fuse together. This mutation can lead to melanoma.

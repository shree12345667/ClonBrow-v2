Neural Resistance

Explore how the Myelin Sheath acts as electrical insulation for nerve axons using Ohm's Law.

Ohm's Law Analogy:
$V = I \times R_{membrane}$
Healthy myelin creates high membrane resistance ($R_m$), preventing current ($I$) leak, allowing the voltage signal ($V$) to travel fast and far.
Status: Healthy Nerve
Myelin is intact. High membrane resistance prevents current leak. Signal propagation (Saltatory Conduction) is optimal.
Pathology: In demyelinating diseases like Multiple Sclerosis (MS), the immune system attacks myelin. Resistance drops, current leaks out, and the signal slows down or fails entirely.

1. Resting State

Potential: -70 mV
Gates: Both Closed

The Na⁺/K⁺ pump maintains the resting potential. The membrane is polarized.

2. Depolarization

Potential: -55 to +40 mV
Gates: Na⁺ Open, K⁺ Closed

Stimulus hits threshold. Voltage-gated Na⁺ channels open. Na⁺ rushes IN, making the inside positive.

3. Repolarization

Potential: +40 to -80 mV
Gates: Na⁺ Inactive, K⁺ Open

Na⁺ channels inactivate. Voltage-gated K⁺ channels open. K⁺ rushes OUT, restoring negative charge.

4. Hyperpolarization

Potential: ~ -80 mV to -70 mV
Gates: K⁺ Closing

K⁺ channels are slow to close, causing an "overshoot". The Na⁺/K⁺ pump restores the resting state.

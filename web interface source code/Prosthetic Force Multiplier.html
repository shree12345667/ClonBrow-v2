Block & Tackle Principle

Fout = Fin × nropes

By adding pulleys, we increase the number of supporting cable segments ($n$). This reduces the tension required from the patient's muscle to lift a heavy object, effectively multiplying their limited strength.

Clinical Application

This is critical for upper-limb amputees using "body-powered" prostheses, where shoulder shrugs or chest expansion provide the only source of mechanical energy.

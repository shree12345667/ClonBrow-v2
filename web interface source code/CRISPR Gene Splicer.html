CRISPR Editing Protocol

1. Recognition The Cas9 enzyme scans the DNA for a PAM sequence (NGG). Once found, the gRNA attempts to unzip and bind to the target.
2. Cleavage Two active nuclease domains (RuvC and HNH) generate a double-strand break precisely 3 base pairs upstream of the PAM.
3. Repair Instead of error-prone NHEJ, we provide a Repair Template. The cell uses Homology-Directed Repair to stitch the healthy gene into the gap.

Sustained-Release Tablet Architecture

A cross-sectional view of a multi-layer modified-release oral dosage form. This design ensures a rapid initial therapeutic effect followed by a prolonged, steady release of the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) over 12 to 24 hours.

Cross-Sectional View

Hover/Click Layers

1. Immediate Release Coat

0 - 1 Hour

The outermost layer dissolves instantly upon reaching the stomach. This provides an initial burst of the drug, ensuring rapid onset of symptom relief.

2. Polymer Barrier / Enteric Coat

Protection

A semi-permeable or pH-sensitive polymer film. It prevents the inner layers from dissolving in acidic gastric juices, delaying release until the tablet reaches the intestines.

3. Extended Release Matrix

2 - 12 Hours

The drug is embedded in a hydrophilic polymer matrix. As fluid enters, the matrix swells and forms a gel layer, allowing the drug to slowly diffuse out at a controlled rate.

4. Drug Core / Maintenance

12 - 24 Hours

The dense innermost core holds the final maintenance dose. Sometimes designed as an insoluble "ghost matrix" that is eventually excreted after the entire drug load has been depleted.

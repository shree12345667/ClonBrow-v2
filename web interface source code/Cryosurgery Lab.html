Latent Heat of Fusion

When the tissue temperature reaches ~0°C, cooling stalls. The thermal energy being removed is now used to convert liquid water into solid ice (breaking hydrogen bonds). This is the Phase Change Plateau.

Mechanism of Necrosis

Once fully frozen, temperature plummets. Sharp intracellular ice crystals physically puncture cell membranes. Below -20°C, cell viability rapidly drops to 0%, destroying the lesion.

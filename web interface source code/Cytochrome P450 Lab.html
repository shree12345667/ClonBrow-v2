Phase I Biotransformation

Drug
Metabolite

CYP450 enzymes (mainly CYP3A4 and CYP2D6) add polar groups (like -OH) to lipophilic molecules. This makes them easier for the kidneys to filter and excrete, preventing systemic toxicity.

Clinical Note:

Grapefruit juice inhibits CYP3A4, slowing the breakdown of many drugs and potentially causing toxic buildup.

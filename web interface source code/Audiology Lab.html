Physics of Sound

Frequency ($Hz$) dictates the pitch of the sound (wavelength). Amplitude ($dB$) dictates the loudness (wave height). The human ear can theoretically detect frequencies from 20 Hz up to 20,000 Hz.

Sensorineural Hearing Loss

Hair cells at the base of the cochlea process high frequencies and are most susceptible to damage from loud noise and aging. Once these cells die, high-pitched sounds vanish permanently.

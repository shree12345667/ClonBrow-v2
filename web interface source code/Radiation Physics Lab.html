Radiation Mechanics

Inverse Square Law ($I = I_0 / r^2$) Because radiation is emitted isotropically (in all directions) like a sphere, the surface area it covers grows with the square of the distance. Therefore, moving twice as far away reduces exposure to 1/4th.
Radioactive Decay ($t_{1/2}$) Isotopes are unstable. Cesium-137 has a half-life of ~30 years. Every 30 years, half of the remaining atoms decay into stable Barium, permanently reducing the source activity $A_0$.

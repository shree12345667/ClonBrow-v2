Wound Dehiscence

Successful suturing requires bringing edges into light contact ("Approximation") without cutting into the tissue.

Optimal Zone (2-8 N) Edges meet perfectly. Blood flow is maintained. Wound healing can proceed.
Ischemic Zone (9-12 N) Sutures "strangle" the tissue. Skin turns pale. Risk of necrosis increases.
Failure Zone (>12.5 N) The mechanical load exceeds the tissue strength. The skin rips red and jagged.

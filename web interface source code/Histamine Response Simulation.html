Histamine Lab

When allergens enter the body, Mast Cells detect them and release Histamine. This chemical signal causes blood vessels to dilate (widen), allowing immune cells to reach the site faster, but also causing redness and swelling.

System: Baseline
Vessel tone is normal. Mast cells are in standby mode. No allergens detected.
Physiology: Vasodilation increases the volume of blood in the area, but the velocity of individual cells actually slows down as the pipe widens, facilitating "leaking" into tissues (edema).

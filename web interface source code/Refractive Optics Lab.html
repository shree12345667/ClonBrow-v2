Vision Correction Physics

Myopia (Nearsighted)

Cornea is too steep. Light converges before the retina. Fixed with a diverging (concave) lens or by flattening the central cornea with a laser.

Hyperopia (Farsighted)

Cornea is too flat. Light converges behind the retina. Fixed with a converging (convex) lens or by steepening the central cornea with a laser.

"Red Hacker" Excimer Laser (LASIK/PRK)

Instead of an external lens, ultraviolet laser pulses (193nm) vaporize specific patterns of corneal tissue to permanently alter its refractive power.

Atelectasis (Collapse)

High pressure in Alveolus A forces air into Alveolus B. The smaller sac collapses completely. Common in premature infants lacking surfactant (RDS).

Law of Laplace

P = 2γ / r
Without Surfactant (Pure Water)

Surface tension ($\gamma$) is high and constant. If radius ($r$) halves during exhalation, pressure ($P$) doubles. Small alveoli empty into large ones.

With Surfactant (Phospholipids)

As the alveolus shrinks, surfactant molecules pack closer together, massively dropping $\gamma$. This counteracts the shrinking $r$, keeping $P$ equal across all alveoli.

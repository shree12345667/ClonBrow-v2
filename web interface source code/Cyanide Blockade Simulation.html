Cyanide Blockade

Mitochondria produce ATP (cellular energy) via the Electron Transport Chain (ETC). Cyanide is a deadly inhibitor that binds to Cytochrome c oxidase, effectively jamming the mechanical-chemical gears of life.

Status: Dormant
The cell is currently in a metabolic resting state.

Mechanism: Cyanide competes with Oxygen to bind at the iron center of Complex IV. This halts electron flow, stops the proton pump, and prevents ATP synthesis.

Clinical Staging

Normothermia (36.5 - 37.5°C) Heat production equals heat loss. Peripheral blood flow is maintained.
Mild Hypothermia (35 - 32°C) Maximal shivering thermogenesis. Severe peripheral vasoconstriction sacrifices the limbs to keep the core warm.
Moderate Hypothermia (32 - 28°C) Shivering completely stops. Confusion sets in. ECG shows bradycardia and characteristic "Osborn J-waves".
Severe Hypothermia (< 28°C) Loss of consciousness. High risk of Ventricular Fibrillation and cardiac arrest as the myocardium cools.

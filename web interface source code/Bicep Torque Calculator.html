Torque Equation

τ = F · r · sin(θ)

Torque is the rotational equivalent of linear force. In a bicep curl, the load torque is highest when the forearm is parallel to the floor (90° flexion), as the horizontal distance (moment arm) from the elbow is maximized.

Mechanical Advantage

Because the bicep inserts so close to the joint (~4cm) compared to the weight in the hand (~35cm), the muscle must exert roughly 8-10x more force than the weight of the object.

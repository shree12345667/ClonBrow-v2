Anthracosis & Deposition

Unlike larger dust trapped by mucus in the throat, PM2.5 particles are so small they ride the air current deep into the terminal alveoli.

Brownian Deposition In the terminal sacs, air velocity drops to near zero. Particles drift randomly until they collide with and permanently lodge into the moist membrane walls.
Tissue Necrosis Macrophages attempt to consume the soot but cannot digest carbon. Over time, the tissue physically darkens (Anthracosis), scars, and loses oxygen exchange capacity.

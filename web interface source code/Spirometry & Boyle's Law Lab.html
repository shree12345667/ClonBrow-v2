Boyle's Law in Action

Boyle’s Law states that pressure is inversely proportional to volume ($P \propto 1/V$).

  • Inhalation: Diaphragm drops $\rightarrow$ Volume increases $\rightarrow$ Intrapulmonary pressure drops below 0 $\rightarrow$ Air is sucked in.
  • Exhalation: Diaphragm relaxes $\rightarrow$ Volume decreases $\rightarrow$ Pressure rises above 0 $\rightarrow$ Air is pushed out.
